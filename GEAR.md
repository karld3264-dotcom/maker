## Outfits by use

- **Riding**: saddle, pad, bridle, noseband, reins, breast, legs (needs: saddle)
- **Western riding**: saddle, pad, bridle, reins, breast, legs
- **Racing**: saddle, pad, bridle, noseband, blinkers, reins, legs (needs: saddle)
- **Driving (carriage)**: harness, bridle, blinkers, reins, legs (needs: harness)
- **Free turnout**: bridle, blanket, head, legs
- **Show**: saddle, pad, bridle, noseband, reins, head, legs
- **Pack / trekking**: saddle, pad, bridle, breast, legs (needs: saddle)
- **Knight / medieval**: saddle, bridle, reins, cover, plate
- **Stable / rest**: bridle, blanket, legs
- **Insect protection**: blanket, head, legs
